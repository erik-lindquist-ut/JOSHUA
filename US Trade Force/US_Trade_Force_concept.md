# U.S. Trade Force — a one-page concept

*Draft. Not sent, shared or published. My own idea, with no tie to any person, party or office.*

**What it is.** U.S. Trade Force is my plan to make useful things here, close to the people who use them, and to get them the last few yards to the door.

**The problem.** A lot of what people need is made far away and shipped a long way. Big, bulky things cost more to ship than to make. And even when a thing gets close, it often fails in the last gap before it reaches a person. In the mountain West, that gap is the widest, because the towns are small and the miles are long.

**Why the name fits now.** President Trump's stated trade policy says it should put "American workers, manufacturers, farmers, ranchers, entrepreneurs, and businesses" first, and it calls for tools like tariffs and new one-on-one trade deals (White House, "America First Trade Policy" memo, Jan. 20, 2025). My plan is not about policy. It is about building things and moving things here.

**Who it helps.** People who make things in U.S. shops. Workers who want new skills. Schools, libraries and venues that buy what I make. And people in ten mountain-West states who live past the end of the line.

**What I do first (small and real).** I get one RIBBON table segment made by one U.S. shop, near where it will be used. I write down where every part comes from and what it cost to ship. That one sheet shows if "made near the buyer" works.

**How my work feeds it.**

- **RIBBON** (PRDs and business plan): my table system is built to be made in small shops near the buyer, from a file, so bulky freight stays low. It is the "make it here" piece.
- **Last Ten Yards** (post 1, twelve-episode plan, web store app): my plan for closing the last gap for people in ten mountain-West states. It is the "get it there" piece.
- **Depot brief:** a site I scouted in Salt Lake County with garages that could be a charge-and-dock home base. The roof, bays and power still need to be measured.
- **Armored drill books:** my study books for work tools. They can train the people who do the work.
- **Patent claims C1–C6:** my written ideas for parts like the docking latch, the seam caps and the service hub. None are filed yet.
