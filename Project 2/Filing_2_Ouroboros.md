# Filing packet 2 of 4 — Ouroboros

*Form TX, mailed. Tick as you go. Not legal advice — this is the Copyright Office's own published procedure.*

## What goes in this envelope — three things, together

| | Item |
|---|---|
| ☐ | **Form TX**, completed and signed in ink |
| ☐ | **Deposit** — `Ouroboros.pdf` printed, **7 pages** |
| ☐ | **Check or money order, $125**, payable to *Register of Copyrights* |

Nothing else. No cover letter, no service request number, no cash.

## Form TX — fill exactly this

| Space | What to write |
|---|---|
| **1** Title | `Ouroboros` — must match the deposit's first page exactly |
| **2** Author | Erik Daniel Lindquist |
| **2** Work made for hire? | **No** |
| **2** Citizen of / domiciled in | United States |
| **2** Anonymous / Pseudonymous | **No** / **No** |
| **2** Nature of authorship | `Text (the author's questions), selection, and arrangement.` |
| **3a** Year completed | **2026** |
| **3b** First publication | **leave blank** — this work is unpublished |
| **4** Claimant | Erik Daniel Lindquist, same address |
| **5** Previous registration | **No** |
| **6a** Preexisting material | `Answer summaries and annotations generated by artificial intelligence.` |
| **6b** Material added | `The author's questions; selection and arrangement.` |
| **7** Correspondence | your name, address, email, phone |
| **8** Certification | check **author** · **sign in ink** · date |
| **9** Mail certificate to | your address |

**Space 3b is the one people get wrong.** Leaving it blank is what makes the work unpublished. Filling it in changes the claim.
