## What it is
A way of writing a long trail of tasks so that a person who doesn't understand the implications of any of them can still walk it. Born 2026-09-21 from his complaint about his own punch list: *"Each carries a task forcing me to make choices I don't understand the implications of. Step 1 has several by itself and I have no idea how each impacts Step 2."*

## The form — the method, and it is his
Every task is **one action**. If the action contains a choice, the choice is **named**, the options are **listed**, the **default** is marked (the smallest correct one — v7), and **what it does to the next step** is written out. Every task ends with **Lost? →** the one page to open, and **Done when** — the paper in your hand. Tasks run strictly in order; task N needs only N−1; stopping at any task costs nothing below it. Anything not needed yet is a **gate** with the one condition that opens it, kept off the page.

## Why it's a product
His hint, same day: a competency-based university's students carry on the order of ~4,000 tasks from enrollment to graduation, academic competencies included, and would want a vivid trail, cradle to grave. The form above is domain-agnostic — it was written for filing an LLC and a nonprofit, and it works unchanged for a degree plan, a licence, a move, a case. **The method travels; no institution's content ever does.**
