# Financing the depot — 7045 S 300 E, Midvale — the brief for tomorrow

*Not advice. Eligibility facts are from the SBA's own page and lender guides as of 2026-09-19; every lender re-checks them. The entity decision is his.*

## The instinct, checked: SBA 504

**A 504 does not fit a nonprofit.** SBA rules exclude businesses engaged in nonprofit, passive or speculative activity. It also requires the borrower to occupy **51%** of an existing building (61% new construction) — a fleet depot with sixteen bays occupied by the operating company would meet that; a rental complex would not. And since March 1, 2026, every direct and indirect owner must be a U.S. citizen or national resident in the U.S.

So the 504 question is really an **entity** question. Two structures, one decision:

| Structure | What it buys | What finances it |
|---|---|---|
| **A · For-profit operating company owns the depot** (the fleet company — pharmadash / the last-ten-yards LLC) | SBA 504: ~10% down, 50% bank first lien, 40% CDC debenture, fixed 20–25 year rate; the company must occupy 51% — sixteen bays, the charging plant, the dispatch office would | The fleet's revenue plus a DPS/venue contract as the debt-service story |
| **B · Nonprofit owns the depot** (the public-safety mission — closing-time rides, DUI reduction — as the charitable purpose) | **Not 504.** CDFI community-facility loans (Utah has six CDFIs; Rocky Mountain Community Reinvestment Corporation and Salt Lake Neighborhood Housing Services lend on community property), HUD-backed and state programs, tax-exempt bonds at scale, and grants for the capital stack | Highway-safety and public-health grants; a lease from the for-profit fleet company for the bays |
| **Both — the common shape** | Nonprofit holds the mission and the grants; for-profit holds the fleet and the operating risk; the depot sits with whichever one can borrow cheapest and occupy 51% | The nonprofit leases bays to the fleet; the lease is the nonprofit's debt service |

## Tomorrow's three calls, in order

1. **A CDC** — the Certified Development Company that packages 504s in Utah (the SBA's 504 page lists them by state). One question: *for-profit fleet depot, 51% occupancy, sixteen bays plus solar and storage — does the project pencil, and what is the down payment on a ~$X purchase?* That answers Structure A in fifteen minutes.
2. **A Utah CDFI** — Rocky Mountain Community Reinvestment Corporation or Salt Lake Neighborhood Housing Services. One question: *a nonprofit acquiring a Midvale property as a community facility for a public-safety fleet — what do you lend on, at what LTV, and what does the board need to show?* That answers Structure B.
3. **The seller** — the complex is listed for rent, not sale; "built by the owner." Whether it is for sale at all is the first number, and it is not on Zillow.
