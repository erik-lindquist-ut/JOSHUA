# Bars by county — from the public licensee list

- Utah Department of Alcoholic Beverage Services publishes licensee lists (bar, restaurant, club) — public.
- Pull **bar licences** for Salt Lake County and Tooele County; add address and closing time (statutory last call 1 a.m.).
- Count per county; map the Salt Lake ones by cluster (downtown, Sugar House, Midvale/Sandy) — clusters, not bars, decide where a car waits.
- Deliverable: `bars.csv` — name, address, county, cluster. The cluster count is what sizes the fleet (≈ one car per cluster per closing window to start).
